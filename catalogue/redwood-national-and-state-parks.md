## New material found in research

- 'O Rew: a 125-acre property on Prairie Creek near Orick, in the center of Yurok ancestral territory, that the Yurok Tribe, Save the Redwoods League, NPS, and CA State Parks agreed on March 19, 2024 to transfer to the Yurok Tribe (transfer to complete in 2026 after restoration), to become a co-stewarded 'gateway' to the parks with a visitor center and traditional village. Quotes: Yurok Chairman Joseph L. James, Save the Redwoods League President Sam Hodder, RNSP Superintendent Steve Mietz. (NPS press release)
- Hyperion, discovered in 2006 and identified by NPS as the world's tallest tree, is in a closed area off-trail; NPS states hiking within the closure 'could result in a fine or jail,' and documents that visitor traffic degraded the tree's base and killed surrounding vegetation, with trash and human waste left behind. NPS does not publish the exact height or coordinates on its public page. (NPS)
- Save-the-Redwoods League was founded in 1918 by three paleontologists: Henry Fairfield Osborn, Madison Grant, and John C. Merriam; between 1920 and 1960 the League purchased over 100,000 acres of redwood forest, forming the basis of Jedediah Smith, Del Norte Coast, and Prairie Creek Redwoods State Parks. (NPS)
- Redwood National Park was signed into law by President Lyndon B. Johnson on October 2, 1968 (58,000 acres, including Lady Bird Johnson Grove and Tall Trees Grove); the 1978 expansion, approved by President Jimmy Carter in March 1978, added 48,000 acres -- of which roughly 39,000 acres had already been clear-cut and required restoration. (NPS)
- By 1853 nine sawmills were already operating in Eureka; by the 1960s industrial logging had removed 'almost ninety percent' of the original ~2,000,000 acres of old-growth redwood, leaving about 5 percent (roughly 100,000 acres) today. (NPS Area History)
- Del Norte Coast Redwoods State Park was established in 1925 with approximately 125 acres. (California State Parks)
- Peer-reviewed field research (Dawson, Oecologia 1998) found fog-derived water made up 13-45% of annual transpiration in Sequoia sempervirens, and that interception/drip from redwood canopies contributed about 34% of the forest's annual hydrologic input versus 17% without trees present -- a more precise, source-traceable figure than a flat '40% of water needs.'
- UNESCO's official World Heritage datasheet gives the inscribed property as 41,571 hectares, including 16,442 hectares of old-growth redwood forest, inscribed in 1980 under criteria (vii) and (ix); California State Parks confirms the parks were named a UNESCO International Biosphere Reserve in 1983.
- NPS (Lady Bird Johnson Grove trail signage) states most redwoods there are 600-800 years old, with some individuals surviving 'two millennia' -- more precise than a blanket 'over 2,000 years.'
- Under the joint 1994 agreement, NPS and California State Parks cooperatively manage Redwood National Park plus Jedediah Smith, Del Norte Coast, and Prairie Creek Redwoods State Parks as a single administrative unit protecting more than 105,000 acres of old- and second-growth forest. (NPS; California State Parks)
