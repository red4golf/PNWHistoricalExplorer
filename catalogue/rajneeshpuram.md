## New material found in research

- Big Muddy Ranch spanned nearly 70,000 acres across Wasco and Jefferson counties, purchased in 1981 for $5.75 million (Oregon Encyclopedia, 'City of Antelope and Muddy Ranch').
- The Rajneeshees took political control of Antelope by moving in and simply outvoting the roughly fifty existing residents in town elections, gaining control of the town council, store, and school (Oregon Encyclopedia).
- The Dalles salmonella attack (Sept 9-Oct 10, 1984) sickened 751 people (388 culture-confirmed, 363 clinical), hospitalized at least 45, and caused no deaths; it targeted salad bars at 10 restaurants, using Salmonella Typhimurium matched to a strain found in a commune laboratory (JAMA/Torok et al. 1997, peer-reviewed).
- Ma Anand Sheela pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit illegal wiretapping and to tamper with a consumer product, and was sentenced to 4.5 years in federal custody on July 22, 1986; co-defendant Ma Anand Puja (Diane Onang) received an identical sentence (United States v. Sheela, 667 F. Supp. 724 (D. Or. 1987), primary/official federal court record).
- Rajneesh was arrested around 2 a.m. on October 28, 1985, when federal agents intercepted two chartered jets during a refueling stop in Charlotte, North Carolina; he and 12 others were taken into custody on a 35-count federal indictment covering conspiracy to violate immigration law, false statements to the INS, harboring illegal aliens, and unlawful flight to avoid prosecution (Washington Post, Oct. 29, 1985, editorial secondary/contemporary news record).
- Ma Anand Sheela and other leaders abruptly departed the commune on September 14, 1985, precipitating its collapse; the commune's 1985 incorporation as a city was later ruled to have been illegal, and by 1986 the site had become a ghost town (Oregon Encyclopedia, 'Rajneeshees').
